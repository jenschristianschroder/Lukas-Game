EXPEDITION BRIEF / 001

Go in quiet.
Come out alive.

Recover all three amber relics, then return to the cyan lift before your oxygen runs out.

01

Listen. Look. Remember. Sonar reveals nearby corridors for a few seconds. It also draws the blind creature to where you fired it. Pulse, then move away.

02

Keep your footsteps light. Hold Shift or toggle Quiet to sneak. The creature hears normal footsteps nearby. Your faint trail helps you retrace your route.

03

Give it something else to chase. Decoys make noise ahead of the direction you face, even around corners. Drop one, then go the other way. You start with three.

04

Two people. One expedition. Co-op shares one screen: the runner moves; the operator controls sonar and decoys. Talk to each other. Both roles can use touch at once.
